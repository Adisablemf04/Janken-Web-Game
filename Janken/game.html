GANKEN

LEVEL 1

  • Classic Rock Paper Scissors.
  • You battle 1 NPC.
  • First to 3 points wins.
  • Win to unlock Level 2.

LEVEL 2

  • Classic Rock Paper Scissors.
  • You battle 1 NPC.
  • First to 3 points wins.
  • Abilities become available.
  • Win to unlock Level 3.

LEVEL 3

  • Three-player battle.
  • You face NPC 1 and NPC 2.
  • If one beats both, they get 1 point.
  • If two tie with the winning sign, they duel.
  • First to 3 points wins.
  • Win to unlock Level 4.

WASD / ARROW KEYS — MOVE
ESC — EXIT ROOM
E — INTERACT

LEVEL 4

  • Two-step round system.
  • You choose twice in one round.
  • Some symbols become unavailable after use.
  • Win to finish the four levels.

ABILITIES

  • Lucky Shield: 51% chance to block 1 defeat.
  • Mind Trick: Chance to force the opponent's result.
  • Direct Strike: Removes 1 NPC point instantly.
  • Each ability can only be used once per match.
