Chapter 6: A Life for a Life

I made it. After everything I had endured, I finally had enough money to pay for Kareena's heart transplant. I rushed to the hospital, handed over everything I had earned and begged the doctors to save my daughter. They immediately prepared her for surgery, assuring me that they would do everything they could. For the first time since that horrible night, I felt something I had almost forgotten.
Hope.
But as the operation began, a terrible headache struck me. At first, I ignored it, believing it was simply exhaustion. Yet with every passing minute, the pain grew worse. The hospital lights began flickering. The voices around me became distorted, and strange memories started flashing before my eyes.
Memories I couldn't recognise.
A bloodstained kitchen.
A familiar voice begging for mercy.
And someone laughing.
Then everything went black.

Chapter 7: The Betrayal

I found myself standing inside my old house. Everything looked exactly as it had before the murders. The furniture, the photographs, even the smell of my partner's perfume. But something was wrong. I could hear an argument coming from the bedroom. My partner was speaking to someone, telling them things I was never meant to hear. She had betrayed me. And the children I had spent my entire life raising...
None of them were mine.
I remembered confronting her. I remembered the humiliation, the anger and the overwhelming feeling that my entire life had been a lie. But then something even more terrifying returned to me. The screams. The night my family was murdered. Only this time, I was no longer remembering the events through the eyes of a victim.
I was remembering them through the eyes of the murderer.

Chapter 8: The Face of Grotesquerie

I stood before a mirror, staring at someone I barely recognised. The face looking back at me was my own. Yet the expression was unfamiliar. A twisted smile appeared across my reflection, and a voice whispered inside my head.
"You finally remember."
I began seeing fragments of that horrible night. My family's terror, the torture, the unbearable suffering. I had spent so long believing Grotesquerie was the monster responsible for destroying everything I loved. But the memories were showing me something else. Grotesquerie wasn't another person.
Grotesquerie was me.
I remembered feeling as though someone else had taken control of my body. Another identity, another consciousness, one capable of committing atrocities I could never imagine doing myself. Had my mind fractured so completely that I could no longer recognise my own actions? Or was I inventing another explanation because I couldn't bear the truth? I couldn't tell which memories belonged to whom anymore.
Then I remembered Kareena.
And my heart sank.

Chapter 9: The Daughter I Failed Twice

I woke on the hospital floor. The corridors were empty. The lights flickered above me, and the smell of disinfectant had been replaced by something metallic and sickening. I stumbled towards Kareena's room. The door was open. What I saw inside made my legs give way.
Kareena was mounted high against the wall, close to the ceiling, in a grotesque imitation of a crucifixion. Her arms were stretched wide, fixed in place as though someone had deliberately arranged her body into a blasphemous religious display.
Her lower body was gone.
She had been severed at the waist, leaving only her upper torso suspended against the wall. The open abdominal cavity exposed the devastation beneath, with torn tissue and coils of intestine hanging down from the mutilated remains. Blood had stained the wall beneath her, spreading across the floor in dark, uneven pools. I couldn't breathe. I had just saved her. I had given everything to bring her back.
Yet here she was. Destroyed in the same monstrous fashion as the family I had failed to protect. I fell to my knees, screaming her name until my throat burned.
Then I noticed something.
My hands.
They were stained with blood.
My clothes were soaked.
And on the hospital window, reflected in the darkness behind me, stood a familiar figure. Grotesquerie. I turned around. Nobody was there. I looked back at the reflection. It was me. My mouth slowly twisted into the same horrible smile I had seen before. And a voice whispered inside my head.
"You can never save someone from yourself."

Chapter 10: The Final Escape

I couldn't understand why I was still alive. My partner. My children. Kareena. Everyone I had loved had suffered because of me. The gambling, the murders, the lies. I had spent so long searching for someone to blame that I never considered the possibility that the monster had been hiding inside my own mind. I wanted the memories to disappear. I wanted the voices to stop. I wanted everything to end. The hospital was silent. The image of Kareena's mutilated body refused to leave my thoughts. I couldn't escape what I had seen. I couldn't escape what I believed I had done. And I could no longer escape myself. Overwhelmed by guilt and despair, I hanged myself. As consciousness slipped away, the hospital faded into darkness. But instead of silence, I heard something familiar. A heartbeat monitor. Then a voice.
"He's responding!" Another voice followed.
"Call the doctor! He's waking up!"

Chapter 11: The Truth

I opened my eyes. White walls. Medical equipment. A tube attached to my arm. I was lying in a hospital bed. A doctor stood beside me, asking whether I could hear him. I tried to speak, but my throat was dry, and my body felt unfamiliar. He told me I had been unconscious for a long time. I had been in a coma. Everything came flooding back. Kareena's operation. The murders. Grotesquerie. The gambling tables. SATAN. I desperately asked about my daughter. The doctor looked confused. Kareena was alive. There had been no heart transplant. No serial killer. No Grotesquerie. The horrifying events I had experienced were constructions of my unconscious mind. But that didn't mean I had nothing to remember.

Chapter 12: The Man Behind the Nightmare

I was never betrayed by my partner. I was the one who betrayed her. I had been unfaithful throughout our relationship, lying to the very people who trusted me most. When my partner discovered the truth, she left me. Our marriage ended. Our family fell apart. And I was left alone with the consequences of my own actions. The children I had believed weren't mine were always mine. The murders had never happened. My mind had transformed my own betrayal into hers, turning my shame into anger and my guilt into an imaginary monster. Grotesquerie was not a hidden murderer living inside me. He was the embodiment of everything I hated about myself. Unable to accept that I had destroyed my own family, I fell into a deep depression. Eventually, I attempted to end my life.
I had hanged myself in reality, just as I had done inside the nightmare.
But someone found me.
Kareena.
My daughter was the one who saved me that day. She managed to get help before it was too late, but I had already suffered serious injuries and lost consciousness. That was how I entered the coma. The same daughter I had spent an eternity trying to save inside GANKEN... Was the person who had saved me in reality.
I stared at the hospital ceiling, listening to the steady rhythm of the machines.
I was alive.
But I had never felt so empty.
I had finally escaped GANKEN.
But at what cost?
